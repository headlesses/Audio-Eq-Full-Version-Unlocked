## Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

Audio EQ is a sound equalizer extension designed specifically for Google Chrome. It allows users to modify high, low, and medium frequencies in HTML5 music players, enhancing the audio experience on various websites such as YouTube and Grooveshark. With Audio EQ, users can overcome the limitations of low sound adjustments typically found in Chrome, providing a tailored audio experience that stands out compared to typical alternatives.
